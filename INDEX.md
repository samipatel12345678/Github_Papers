## ✨ What This System Does

```
You provide a topic (e.g., "AI in Healthcare")
        ↓
Researcher gathers information
        ↓
Writer creates engaging article
        ↓
Critic reviews and provides feedback
        ↓
Feedback processor decides:
  Continue with improvements? → Loop back
  Or finish? → Return final article
        ↓
Article ready for publication!
```
